OneSeerah for iPhone and iPad

Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the OneSeerah iOS app from Zeeq AI Solutions.

Reading progress

The app saves your reading position and the parts you mark as read on your device. The app does not send this data to us.

You can read without an account. OneSeerah does not use ads, analytics, or tracking in the app.

Delete reading progress

OneSeerah keeps your reading progress until you clear it or delete the app. To clear it, open Settings on the Readings tab. Tap Clear Reading Progress. Tap Clear Progress to confirm.

External references

If you open an original reference, your browser goes to another website. That site's privacy policy applies to your visit.

Support requests

If you email us, we use your email address and message to answer your request. Contact us to ask about your data or request deletion.
