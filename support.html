Reading help

How do I find a reading?

Open Readings. Search for a title, such as Cave Hira. Tap the result to open it. Use Next part to continue.

Where can I see the source or timeline?

In a reading, tap a reference under Sources to see its summary. Tap Open original reference to visit the source website.

To find the event on the timeline, return to the reading and tap Show on timeline.

Can I read offline?

Yes. You can use the readings, timeline, and source summaries offline without an account. External websites need an internet connection.

How do I resume or clear my progress?

On Readings, tap Continue reading to return to your saved place.

To remove all saved positions and marks on this device, open Settings on the Readings tab. Tap Clear Reading Progress. Tap Clear Progress to confirm.

Report a problem or correction

For a story or source correction, include the reading title, the passage, and the reference you want us to review.

For an app problem, describe what happened. Include your device model and your iOS or iPadOS version.
